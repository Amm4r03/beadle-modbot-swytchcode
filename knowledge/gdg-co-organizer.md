# How to become a GDG co-organizer

To become a co-organizer: attend consistently, volunteer at events, then ask the organizer team directly — chapters list organizers on their page. The path is regular attendance, then volunteering, then a direct ask. Organizer work is logistics (venue, food, registrations, speakers) plus keeping the welcome warm. Starting a new chapter where none exists goes through "Lead a GDG" (students: GDG on Campus track).
