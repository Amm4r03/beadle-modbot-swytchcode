# Watering indoor plants in Bangalore

- Most indoor plants: water when the top 2-3 cm of soil is dry — roughly every 4-6 days in summer, 7-10 in monsoon.
- Bangalore tap water is hard: use filtered water for misting ferns; tap is fine for soil.
- Succulents: every 10-14 days; less in monsoon.
- Overwatering shows as yellow lower leaves; underwatering shows crisp brown edges.
