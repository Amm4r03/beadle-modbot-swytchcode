# Volunteering, speaking, and organizing with GDG

## How to propose a talk (CFP process)

To propose a talk at GDG: find the chapter's contact — organizer profiles on the chapter page, or ask at a meetup — and pitch a concrete topic with what attendees will take away. There is no central GDG CFP form; each chapter runs its own lineup. Organizers favor practitioner talks about real projects on Google stack; first-time speakers are common [INFERENCE from program tone]. A runnable demo beats a slide-deck proposal. Lead time is typically weeks, not months, for monthly meetups; DevFest seasons open formal CFPs announced on chapter channels.
