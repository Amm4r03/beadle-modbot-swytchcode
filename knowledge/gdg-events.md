# GDG event formats and how RSVPs work

Sources: Google for Developers community and DevFest pages (developers.google.com/community, /community/devfest) and chapter event listings on gdg.community.dev.

Typical GDG event formats: (1) Tech talks — 20–45 minute sessions by Googlers, Google Developer Experts, or community members on Android, Cloud, AI/ML, Flutter, Web. (2) Codelabs — guided hands-on labs where attendees build along on their laptops. (3) Study jams — multi-session group learning around a course or certification path. (4) Hackathons and buildathons — e.g. the Build with AI series. (5) Watch parties — livestreamed keynotes (Google I/O Extended) watched together with local discussion. (6) DevFest — the flagship annual full-day conference each chapter may host in the Oct–Dec season, with multiple tracks, workshops, and networking.

How RSVPs work: each event on the chapter page shows date, venue or online link, and a registration button; many chapters use external registration (the button is labeled "External registration"). Seats are limited by venue — RSVP on the event page and watch for confirmation details. Some events are free, larger ones may charge a small fee.
