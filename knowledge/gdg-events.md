# GDG event formats and how RSVPs work

## How RSVPs work (mechanics)

To RSVP: open the event on the chapter page and complete registration there — many chapters label the button "External registration," meaning signup finishes on a linked form or event platform. Venue seats are limited, so RSVP early and watch for confirmation details. No chapter account beyond the free "Join us" membership is needed to RSVP.

How RSVPs work: each event on the chapter page shows date, venue or online link, and a registration button; many chapters use external registration (the button is labeled "External registration"). Seats are limited by venue — RSVP on the event page and watch for confirmation details. Some events are free, larger ones may charge a small fee. ## Event taxonomy at a glance

| Format | What it is | Cadence | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tech talk | 20–45 min session by a Googler, GDE, or member | monthly meetups | free |
| Codelab | guided hands-on lab, build along on your laptop | monthly / study-jam series | free |
| Study jam | multi-session group learning (course/cert path) | seasonal series | free |
| Hackathon / buildathon | timed build event, e.g. Build with AI series | occasional | free |
| Watch party | livestream keynote watched together (e.g. I/O Extended) | around Google events | free |
| DevFest | flagship full-day conference, multi-track + workshops | annual, Oct–Dec season | free or small fee (per-chapter) |
