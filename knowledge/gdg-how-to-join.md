# How to join a GDG chapter and find events

Sources: Google for Developers community hub (developers.google.com/community) and chapter pages on gdg.community.dev. Chapter discovery runs through Google's official directory, not third-party lists.

To join: open the Google for Developers community page and use "Find your community" (the developers-community map) to locate the nearest Google Developer Group. Each chapter has its own page on gdg.community.dev with an About section, upcoming events, past events, organizers, photos, and video. Membership counts are shown per chapter. Joining is typically a one-click "Join us" on the chapter page plus RSVPing to individual events.

To find events: the chapter page lists upcoming events with dates and registration links (many use external registration). The DevFest season site lists city events Oct–Dec each year. Google also runs a global Google Developer Community Discord (discord.com/invite/google-dev-community) for online discussion between meetups.

Practical notes [INFERENCE]: RSVP early — popular codelabs and DevFest city events fill up. Most evening meetups are free; some larger events charge a small fee for venue and catering. Bring a laptop to codelabs; talks need nothing but yourself. If there is no chapter in your city, the same hub links "Lead a GDG" for starting one.
