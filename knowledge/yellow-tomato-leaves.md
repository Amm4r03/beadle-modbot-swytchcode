# Yellow spots on tomato leaves after rain

Yellow halo spots on lower leaves after heavy rain usually mean early blight (Alternaria) or a fungal leaf spot, not a nutrient problem.

What to do:
- Remove the affected lower leaves and discard them (do not compost).
- Water at the base only; keep foliage dry; improve airflow.
- If it spreads, apply a copper-based fungicide or neem oil every 7 days.
- Mulch to stop soil splash.

Bangalore note: after monsoon rains this is common; ~4 hours of sun is fine if the leaves stay dry.
